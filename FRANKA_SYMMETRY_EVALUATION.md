# Symmetry-aware Franka evaluation

`isaac_rl/scripts/benchmark_franka_placement.py` and `scripts/record_franka_policy_videos.py` use finite and validated continuous axial object symmetries by default. Use `--no-symmetry-evaluation` for the previous single-reference score. Other standalone evaluators opt in with `cfg.pose_evaluation = True` and `cfg.symmetry_evaluation = True` before constructing `FrankaZedEnv`. Displays use `env.evaluation_error_norms()`; nominal control errors remain available through `env.pose_errors()`.

Training keeps its original objective and checkpoint/catalog contract. This change does not restart, modify or retrain an existing policy. Rewards and actor input remain nominal; the additional equivalence check is for evaluation termination and reporting.

## Meaning

For object pose O, nominal TCP goal G and object symmetry S, the equivalent TCP is `O S O^-1 G`. The symmetry acts around the object, including its centre offset. Rotating only the TCP orientation would be incorrect for an off-centre grasp.

A candidate passes only when **both** position and angle are within tolerance for that **same** candidate. Selection minimizes the maximum tolerance-normalized error. A different tolerance pair must select again; independently minimizing position and angle can produce false successes.

The existing collision checks and policy completion/stability requirements still apply. Grasp-equivalent alignment does not demonstrate physical pickup, lifting or assembly success.

## Supported symmetry evidence

The evaluator loads the existing per-part `assets/obj/fabrica/*/symmetries.json` records, checks rigid transforms, applies asset-to-bundle mesh scale, and conjugates through the saved source frame. It requires an accepted finite record and a recorded maximum vertex discrepancy of at most **0.1 mm at runtime scale**. Less accurate geometric candidates are excluded explicitly. Identity is always retained.

Source bundle hashes and symmetry-file hashes, accepted names, excluded names, and continuous-hint counts are recorded in `symmetry_evaluation.json`. Missing assets, ambiguous frames, changed source bundles and invalid matrices fail explicitly. Multiple source bundles for one part/orientation are supported only when their target mesh/frame metadata agree.

The v2 profile additionally loads `assets/obj/fabrica/continuous_symmetries.json`, generated by `scripts/validate_franka_continuous_symmetries.py`. Inertia-derived axes are only proposals. The audit rotates all mesh vertices plus 2,048 seeded surface samples at ten non-grid angles and measures point-to-triangle distance, with an explicit **0.15 mm surface/faceting allowance**. This is a sampled geometric check, not a proof of exact invariance of a triangulated mesh. It admits the four car wheels (parts 0, 3, 4, 5); maximum measured mismatch is below 0.144 mm. Hex heads, square features and other rotational details fail the continuous test. Finite candidates at 30-degree increments plus 45/135/225/315 degrees receive their own surface checks under the same allowance; accepted additions recover missed duct fastener rotations and the stool plate's fourfold symmetry. The existing 0.1 mm vertex-record threshold remains unchanged. Surface evidence and mesh hashes are recorded separately, and runtime scale applies to the allowances.

Continuous scoring is genuinely any-angle, not a dense finite approximation. In each finite coset, position and orientation tolerances each define a circular interval of acceptable angles; a float64 bisection finds the minimum shared normalized error. Both distances are recomputed at that same angle. Centre offsets and arbitrary world/source rotations are included. `SymmetryEvaluator.evaluate(...)` handles both kinds; the older `errors(...)` plus `select(...)` API scores finite representatives only. Construct the evaluator with `continuous=[row["continuous"] for row in report["targets"]]` from `catalog_symmetries`.

A generic 180-degree Panda hand flip is not automatically accepted: the camera and surrounding hand geometry are asymmetric. Appearance markings and downstream assembly constraints may also distinguish geometrically equivalent object poses. Use this evaluator for the uniformly colored, static-object alignment task; it rejects dynamic-object mode.

## Results and compatibility

With symmetry evaluation enabled, `position_error_m`, `rotation_error_rad`, `success`, `premature` and `divergence` use symmetry equivalence. `nominal_position_error_m`, `nominal_rotation_error_rad`, `nominal_success` and `symmetry_representative` preserve the comparison to the original goal. Complete `tcp_{x,y,z,qw,qx,qy,qz}` and `goal_*` fields permit later rescoring with a different tolerance pair.

Existing videos/results are not silently relabelled. Earlier recordings contain scalar distances rather than complete terminal poses, so they cannot generally be rescored for symmetry without recovering or replaying the states. For example, a 3.78-degree residual around a beam's nominal pose still fails a 3-degree criterion even if that beam also admits a 180-degree rotation.

## Expanded coverage verification (2026-09-23)

The 439-target validation catalog now has 21 of 34 parts with nonidentity equivalences, 69 finite nonidentity records and continuous axial orbits on 61 wheel targets. These counts are dated observations. The inspector displays all 21 covered parts, including a free-angle wheel slider.

27 targeted CPU tests pass, including arbitrary angles, off-axis TCP displacement, coupled-error rejection, finite cosets, world placement, dense-search comparison, invalid inputs and mesh/frame/scale provenance. The frozen epoch-5000 Isaac GPU smoke passed with 34 environments, 80 steps, 58 completed episodes, 1,048 finite equivalent-pose checks and 244 continuous equivalent-pose checks. Actor privileged-label invariance and the existing checkpoint contract remained unchanged. Evidence is in `artifacts/franka_symmetry_evaluation_20260923/catalog_coverage_v2.json` and `continuous_smoke.json`. This smoke is not a new aggregate policy benchmark.
